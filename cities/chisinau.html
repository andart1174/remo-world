⏳ Chișinău: 1910 vs 2026 Historical Time-Warp

In 1910, Chișinău was an elegant limestone capital designed with wide neoclassical boulevards by architect Alexander Bernardazzi, anchored by the 1841 Triumphal Arch and lush central gardens. By 2026, Chișinău (IATA: RMO) pairs its iconic white-stone City Gates and park-lined avenues with a rapidly expanding European IT park, boutique specialty coffee culture, and world-record wine tourism.
